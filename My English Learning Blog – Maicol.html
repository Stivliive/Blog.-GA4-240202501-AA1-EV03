English grammar in my activities

These are the grammar structures I used in three of my evidences.

Job application email

Grammar structures: Present Continuous, Present Perfect, Modal verb CAN

Example from my workStructure
I am writing to apply for the position of Junior Software Developer.Present Continuous
I am studying a technologist program in Software Analysis and Development at SENA.Present Continuous
I have created a social app, and I am still improving it.Present Perfect
I can speak Spanish and I am learning English.Modal verb CAN
I can offer teamwork, motivation to learn, and a strong desire to never give up.Modal verb CAN

Grammar explanation: I used the Present Continuous to say what I am doing now, like studying and writing the email. I used the Present Perfect to talk about something I started in the past and that is still important now, like my app. I used CAN to talk about my abilities and what I can offer to a company.

Audio: my academic and professional life

Grammar structures: Modal verbs (have to / should / must), Simple Past, Be going to

Example from my workStructure
I have to improve my programming and my English.Modal verb: have to
I should practice speaking English more.Modal verb: should
I must finish my projects.Modal verb: must
I worked at Coordinadora Mercantil as a warehouse assistant.Simple Past
I loaded and unloaded trucks, organized packages, and helped with inventory logistics.Simple Past
Today, I am going to present my audio.Be going to

Grammar explanation: I used modal verbs to talk about obligations and advice: HAVE TO and MUST are obligations, and SHOULD is advice for myself. I used the Simple Past to talk about my seasonal job, because it finished in the past. I used BE GOING TO to talk about my plan for the audio.

Tree of Life presentation

Grammar structures: Verb TO BE, Simple Present, Simple Past

Example from my workStructure
I am 19 years old.Verb TO BE
I am creative and persevering.Verb TO BE
I live in Manizales, Colombia.Simple Present
My family and my friends support me.Simple Present
I grew up with my grandmother, my father, my aunt, and my uncle.Simple Past
I created an app, and I never give up.Simple Past + Simple Present

Grammar explanation: I used the verb TO BE to introduce myself and describe my qualities. I used the Simple Present to talk about where I live and the people who support me. I used the Simple Past to talk about my childhood and the app I made.
