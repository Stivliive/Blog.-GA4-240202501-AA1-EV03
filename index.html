PART 02 · WHAT I’VE LEARNED

English grammar
in my activities.

Three activities, the structures I used, and a short note on why they fit.

01

A JOB APPLICATION EMAIL

Talking about myself and what I can do

Present ContinuousPresent PerfectModal verb: can

  • “I am writing to apply for the position of Junior Software Developer.”PRESENT CONTINUOUS
  • “I have created a social app, and I am still improving it.”PRESENT PERFECT + PRESENT CONTINUOUS
  • “I can speak Spanish and I am learning English.”CAN + PRESENT CONTINUOUS

Grammar explanation
I used the Present Continuous for actions happening now, such as writing and learning. I used the Present Perfect to connect creating my app in the past with its importance today. I used can to describe an ability.

02

MY MINECRAFT STORY

Sharing a memory and its impact

Simple PastSimple Present

  • “When I was 13 years old, I started playing Minecraft.”SIMPLE PAST
  • “I played with my online friends, and we had a lot of fun together.”SIMPLE PAST
  • “Today, I am studying software, and I think that experience was the beginning of everything.”PRESENT CONTINUOUS + SIMPLE PRESENT + SIMPLE PAST

Grammar explanation
I used the Simple Past to tell what happened when I was younger. I used the Simple Present in “I think” to share what I believe about that memory today.

03

MY TREE OF LIFE

Describing who I am and where I’m going

Verb TO BESimple PresentSimple Past

  • “I am creative and persevering.”VERB TO BE
  • “My family and friends support me.”SIMPLE PRESENT
  • “I grew up with my family.”SIMPLE PAST

Grammar explanation
I used the verb to be to describe my qualities. I used the Simple Present for the people who support me now, and the Simple Past to describe my childhood.
